FocusFlow AI - Home Page Image

The home page now includes a student-learning hero image using a Pexels image URL in templates/home.html.
The styling is in static/css/style.css under "Home-page hero with student image".

If you want to use your own image instead of the online image, place it in static/images/students.jpg and replace the <img> src in templates/home.html with:

{{ url_for('static', filename='images/students.jpg') }}
